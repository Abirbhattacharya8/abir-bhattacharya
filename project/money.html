THE BIG IDEA

Why Does Money Exist?

Before money existed, people exchanged goods directly. This system was called barter. As societies grew, bartering became difficult, leading humans to develop different forms of money that were easier to store, carry, measure and exchange.

THE EVOLUTION

Journey Through Time

How humans moved from objects to digital numbers.

STAGE 01

Barter System

People exchanged goods and services directly. For example, a farmer could exchange wheat for clothes.

STAGE 02

Commodity Money

Useful objects such as salt, cattle, grains and shells were used as a medium of exchange.

STAGE 03

Metal Coins

Metals such as gold, silver and copper became popular because they were durable and portable.

STAGE 04

Paper Money

Paper currency made large transactions easier and reduced the need to carry heavy metals.

STAGE 05

Banking & Cheques

Banks introduced systems that allowed people to store money and make payments without physically carrying cash.

STAGE 06

Cards & ATMs

Debit and credit cards made electronic payments and cash withdrawals faster and more convenient.

STAGE 07

Digital Payments

Smartphones and online banking allow people to transfer money instantly without physical cash.

STAGE 08

The Future

The future of money may include advanced digital currencies, programmable payments and new financial technologies.

INDIA

Money in India

From ancient coins to modern digital payments.

🇮🇳 Indian Rupee

The Indian Rupee is the official currency of India. Its symbol is ₹. The currency is issued and managed within India's monetary and banking system.

🏦 Banking

Banks provide services such as deposits, loans, money transfers and electronic payments. They play an important role in the modern economy.

📱 Digital Payments

Digital payment systems have made it possible to transfer money quickly using smartphones and banking applications.

💼 Importance in Commerce

Money acts as a medium of exchange, a measure of value and a store of value, making modern business and trade much easier.
